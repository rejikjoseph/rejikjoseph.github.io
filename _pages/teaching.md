  <h2 class="course-title">International Trade: Theory, Policy and Negotiations</h2>

  <p>I co-teach this course with a colleague. The course introduces the main theories of international trade and how trade policy is made. It gives an overview of multilateral and bilateral trade arrangements, looks at the key public policy issues they raise, and considers the political economy of India’s trade policy.</p>
